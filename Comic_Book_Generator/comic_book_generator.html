Comic Book Generator

Turn a written chapter into a lettered, print-ready comic book. Paste in your manuscript and design bible, and the generator plans every page, draws it, letters the dialogue, and exports the finished book.

From script to comic book

What you get

A production plan for every page

An AI director splits your chapter into 11×17 portrait pages and writes a panel-by-panel art brief, keeping to your design bible's characters, wardrobe and look.

Consistent artwork

Page art is generated with Google's Imagen model from those briefs, with your design bible included in every request.

Clean, accurate lettering

Text recognition finds each numbered balloon, then draws a padded white box and wraps your exact dialogue inside it.

Export anywhere

Compile pages into a PDF for print, a CBZ for comic readers, or one long vertical strip for the web. Convert an existing CBZ to PDF in one click.

How it works

  • Create Chapter: load your manuscript and design bible, and the generator produces every lettered page.
  • Compile Book: pick your finished pages, check the thumbnails, and export.
  • One launcher: a small window with Create Chapter, Compile Book and Quit opens each tool.

Requirements

  • Windows, macOS or Linux with Python 3 (a packaged Windows version is also supported)
  • A Google Gemini API key for the page art
  • Tesseract OCR installed, for finding the balloon IDs
  • Python packages: PySide6, customtkinter, Pillow, python-dotenv, pydantic, google-genai
